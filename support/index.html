Common questions

How do I record a sermon?

On Today, tap Record a sermon and keep your phone near the speaker. When you stop, Gilgal makes a transcript and writes your notes. You can also import an audio file, or a link to a service you watched online.

My notes didn't appear, or the transcript failed.

Check that Gilgal has microphone and speech recognition access in Settings. Very quiet or very long recordings can fail; try again from the sermon's page. If our notes server can't be reached, Gilgal writes simpler notes on your device so you still have something.

Do my sermons sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Yes, when you're signed in to iCloud on each device with iCloud on for Gilgal. Your data goes to your own private iCloud, which we can't see.

What languages does Gilgal support?

English, Spanish, Portuguese and Korean, for recording, notes and the bundled Bibles. Pick the sermon language in Settings.

How do I cancel or restore Gilgal Plus?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Cancel any time in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions. On a new device, open Gilgal Plus and tap Restore purchases.

How do I leave or report something in a circle?

Open the circle and use the menu to leave it (or end it, if you made it). To report a post, use Report a problem in the same menu and tell us what happened.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting a sermon or journal entry removes it from your device and your iCloud. To remove everything, delete the app and, in iCloud settings, delete Gilgal's data.
